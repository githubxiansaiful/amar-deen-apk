# Privacy Policy for আমার দ্বীন (AmarDeen)

**Application Name:** আমার দ্বীন (AmarDeen)  
**Package Name:** `com.amardeen.islamicapp`  
**Last Updated:** November 2026  

---

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy outlines how **আমার দ্বীন (AmarDeen)** ("the App") handles information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. **We do not collect, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to remote servers.**

## 2. Information We Access and How It Is Used
The application requires certain permissions strictly to deliver core Islamic utility features:

* **Location (Precise & Approximate):** Used exclusively on-device to accurately calculate prayer times (Namaz schedules) and determine the exact Qibla compass bearing. Location data never leaves your device.
* **Notifications & Exact Alarms:** Used to alert you when prayer (Salat) and Adhan times arrive.
* **Compass & Sensors:** Used in real-time to orient the interactive Qibla needle toward the Kaaba in Mecca. Sensor data is processed in memory and not logged.
* **Local Storage:** Used solely to persist your offline preferences (selected city, calculation method, madhab, saved Quran verses/bookmarks, tasbih counter records).
* **Internet:** Used strictly to stream audio recitations of the Holy Quran and fetch static content.

## 3. Third-Party Sharing & Advertising
* We do **not** use third-party advertising networks (e.g. AdMob).
* We do **not** sell, trade, or monetize your data.
* No personal analytics trackers are embedded in the app.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
The app is safe and intended for audiences of all ages, including children and families. No information is collected from any users, regardless of age.

## 5. Data Deletion
Since all data is stored exclusively on your device, deleting the app or selecting "Clear Data" in your device settings will permanently remove all stored preferences and bookmarks.

## 6. Contact
For any inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the developer contact email on our Google Play Store listing.
