Privacy Policy

Application: আমার দ্বীন (AmarDeen) • Package ID: com.amardeen.islamicapp
Last Updated: November 2026

Summary: আমার দ্বীন (AmarDeen) is designed with user privacy as a fundamental priority. We do NOT collect, transmit, sell, or monetize any of your personal data. All your religious practices, bookmarks, and preferences remain solely on your device.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how আমার দ্বীন (AmarDeen) ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application on Android devices. We are committed to ensuring transparency and safeguarding user privacy.

2. Data Collection and Usage

The app does not require account registration or collect personally identifiable information (PII). Below are the device capabilities and data accessed by the app:

  • Location Data (Approximate and Precise): Used exclusively on-device to compute astronomical Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, Sunrise, Sahri/Iftar) and to calibrate the Qibla compass pointer toward the Holy Kaaba. Your GPS coordinates are processed locally and are never stored on remote servers, logged, or shared with third parties.
  • Notifications and Exact Alarms: Used solely to deliver scheduled Adhan and prayer reminders at the exact calculated times, even when the app is in the background or device has been restarted.
  • Device Sensors (Compass / Magnetometer / Accelerometer): Read in real-time to compute physical azimuth orientation for the interactive 3D Qibla direction compass. Sensor values are discarded immediately after calculation.
  • Vibration: Used to give tactile feedback during Digital Tasbih counter taps and alarm alerts.
  • Local Storage (DataStore Preferences): Your settings (such as selected city, calculation method, madhab, dark mode, saved Quranic surah bookmarks, and daily amal checklist progress) are stored locally in private application storage on your own device.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The app contains no third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or data-broker integrations. Network access (Internet permission) is used strictly to stream audio recitations of the Holy Quran and fetch static content.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not store your data on external servers, all your bookmarks, preferences, and local data can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the application or using Android's standard Settings → Apps → আমার দ্বীন → Clear Storage.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is completely family-friendly and suitable for all age groups, including children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable by law).

6. Security

We implement industry-standard best practices. Since all sensitive preferences and computations occur locally in your device's isolated application sandbox, your data is shielded from unauthorized external access.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer team through your Google Play developer contact email or repository issue tracker.
